Fundamental Rights

UPSC GS-2APPSC Paper IIHigh importance · often in past papers
Notes In the news · 4 Questions · 25 Videos · 3 My doubts · 2 Sources

Part III of the Constitution (Articles 12–35) lists the Fundamental Rights. They protect individuals against the State and are enforceable in court.

Key points
Right to equality · Articles 14–18
Right to freedom · Articles 19–22
Right against exploitation · Articles 23–24
Freedom of religion · Articles 25–28
Cultural and educational rights · Articles 29–30
Right to constitutional remedies · Article 32
From: NCERT Class 11 ch. 2 · your photo notes of Laxmikanth ch. 7
Must remember
Article 12 defines “State”
Article 17 abolishes untouchability
Article 32 · the “heart and soul” of the Constitution (Ambedkar)
